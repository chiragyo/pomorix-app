Privacy Policy for Pomorix

Effective date: 4 October 2026. This policy explains what information Pomorix handles, why, and the choices and rights you have under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Terms of UseContact

1. Who we are

Pomorix (“Pomorix”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a Pomodoro focus-timer mobile app for Android, developed and operated by an individual developer based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), we act as the Data Fiduciary for the limited personal data described below.

Contact for all privacy matters: pomorixapp@gmail.com

2. Short version

  • Pomorix works without an account, sign-up or login.
  • Your focus sessions, tasks, goals, statistics and settings are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. Analytics can be switched off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • No ads. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Analytics and your choice

  • Analytics are collected only when allowed. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, nothing is collected until you tap “Allow” on the consent prompt. In other regions analytics are on by default and you can turn them off at any time.
  • You can review or change your decision any time at Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; it does not affect earlier lawful processing.
  • Pomorix disables Google advertising IDs, ad storage and ad personalisation for analytics.
  • Analytics are not used to identify you personally, and we do not attempt to re-identify the anonymous data.

5. Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications — to alert you when a focus session or break ends, and for optional daily reminders. Asked only with your approval; you can revoke it in system settings.
  • Exact alarms / run at device start — so timers and reminders fire on time and are restored after a restart.
  • Vibration and keep-screen-awake — to give haptic feedback and keep the screen on during a session if you enable it.
  • Internet and network state — only for analytics, crash reports, in-app update/review prompts and opening links.
  • These permissions are used solely for their stated purpose. We do not read your files; file access for backups happens only through the system file picker, for the file you choose.

6. How we use data

  • To provide, operate and maintain the app and its features.
  • To analyse aggregate usage and diagnose crashes so we can improve stability and design.
  • To respond to support, feedback and privacy requests.
  • To comply with applicable law and to protect our legal rights.

We process data on the basis of your consent (analytics, notifications) or for the legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act, such as responding to you at your request or complying with law.

7. Third-party services and sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app: Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), Google Play (distribution, in-app updates and reviews) and, if you email us, Google Gmail. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies (see policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy). We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties, including websites or apps you reach from links inside Pomorix.

8. International transfers

Firebase may process and store data on servers located outside India. By using the app and, where applicable, allowing analytics, you understand that anonymous usage and diagnostic data may be transferred outside India, subject to the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage or uninstall Pomorix. Uninstalling removes it permanently unless you made a backup.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period under Firebase’s retention settings, after which they are deleted or aggregated.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal requirements.
  • To ask us to delete data we hold about you, email pomorixapp@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to our service providers and minimal data collection. Local data is protected by your device’s own security (screen lock, Android sandboxing). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your device and backup files safe.

11. Children

Pomorix is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children. Under the DPDP Act a “child” is a person under 18. If you are under 18, please use the app only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, do not track or profile them, and show no targeted advertising.

If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact pomorixapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase personal data we hold, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent (for analytics, use Settings → Privacy).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Seek grievance redressal, as described below.

Email pomorixapp@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. We may need to verify your identity, and we aim to respond within a reasonable time, not exceeding any period set by law. Because analytics data is anonymous and not linked to your name or email, we may be unable to locate individual records; in that case we will tell you.

13. Grievance redressal

For any privacy complaint, email pomorixapp@gmail.com with the subject “Grievance”, addressed to the Grievance Officer, Pomorix, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you remain dissatisfied after raising the matter with us, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and operational, as provided under the DPDP Act.

14. Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority in India, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, safety or property, or those of others. We will limit any such disclosure to what is legally required.

15. Data breaches

If a personal data breach affecting you occurs and notification is required by law, we will inform you and the relevant authority in the manner and time prescribed.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the app. Continuing to use Pomorix after an update means you accept the revised policy; if you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.

17. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Subject to any rights you cannot waive under applicable law, the courts at Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or relating to this Policy or your use of the app.
